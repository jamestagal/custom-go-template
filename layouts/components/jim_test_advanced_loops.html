Advanced Loop Patterns

Array Spread in Loop:

{for animal of ["🦄 unicorn", ...animals]}
{animal}
{/for}

Inline Array Iteration:

{for word of ["Waller", "loves Plenti", "uses AI", "is Australian"]}
{name} {word}
{/for} {for phrase of ["rocks", "codes", "innovates"]}
{name} {phrase}!
{/for}
